How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by the administrator of the Immich server. Sign in with the email and password you were given.

How does it work?

Swipe right (or tap Keep) to keep a photo. Swipe left (or tap Delete) to delete it. Every photo and video is backed up to your Immich account in the background. Deleted items go to your Immich trash. Tap Clean Up to remove them from your iPhone. iOS asks you to confirm first.

I deleted something by mistake.

Tap the Undo arrow to bring back the last item you deleted. Older deletes stay in your Immich trash for 30 days and can be restored there.

Photoclean says it needs photo access.

Open Settings → Photoclean → Photos and choose Full Access.
