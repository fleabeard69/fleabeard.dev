Do I need an account?

No. You can swipe and free up space without one. An account is only needed to back up your photos. Accounts are created by the administrator of the Immich server. To sign in, tap the account menu (top left), then Sign In to Back Up, and use the email and password you were given.

How does it work?

Swipe right (or tap Keep) to keep a photo. Swipe left (or tap Delete) to delete it. Tap Clean Up to remove deleted items from your iPhone. iOS asks you to confirm first, and removed items stay in your iPhone's Recently Deleted album for 30 days.

Without an account, nothing is backed up, so Photoclean warns you before Clean Up. When you're signed in, every photo and video is backed up to your Immich account in the background, and deleted items go to your Immich trash before they leave your iPhone.

What's the bar above the buttons?

It fills up as you delete, toward 25. When it's full, Photoclean offers to clean up so you can free up space in one go (you can say Keep Swiping). Undo takes it back down. When you're signed in, it shows "Backing up…" until your deletes are safely in your Immich trash.

How do I turn off the sounds or animations?

Photoclean plays one soft sound when you reach 25 deletes. It follows your iPhone's ring/silent switch, so turn on Silent Mode to mute it. It never stops music or podcasts that are playing. To turn off the hearts, puffs and confetti, turn on Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion.

I deleted something by mistake.

Tap the Undo arrow to bring back the last item you deleted. After Clean Up, removed items stay in your iPhone's Recently Deleted album (Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted) for 30 days. When you're signed in, they're also in your Immich trash for 30 days by default.

Photoclean says it needs photo access.

Open Settings → Photoclean → Photos and choose Full Access.
