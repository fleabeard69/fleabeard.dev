Photoclean Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Photoclean is an iPhone app that backs up your photos and videos to your Immich account and lets you swipe through them to keep or delete them. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What Photoclean handles

Your email address and password. They are sent to the Immich server only to sign you in. The password is not stored on your iPhone. The server returns a sign-in token, which is kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

Your photos and videos. With your permission, Photoclean reads your photo library and uploads your photos and videos, with the information inside them (such as the date taken and, if your camera recorded it, the location), to your Immich account. This is the backup the app exists to make.

Your keep and delete choices. When you delete an item, Photoclean moves it to the trash in your Immich account. The trash keeps items for 30 days by default, so they can be restored. When you tap Clean Up, iOS asks you to confirm before anything is removed from your iPhone.

Where your data goes

Your data goes to one place: the Immich server at immich.massgrave.cloud, operated by [OPERATOR NAME]. It is not sent anywhere else.

Photoclean contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind. Your data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used to profile you.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your photos and videos stay in your Immich account until you delete them. Signing out of Photoclean removes your sign-in token from the iPhone. It doesn't delete anything from your account.

To have your account and everything in it deleted, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

Children

Photoclean accounts are created by the server's administrator. The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them.
